Library Circulation Rule 15-Day Policy

Default Loan Duration: Each member can borrow a book for up to 15 days from the date of issue.

Automatic Restocking: When a book is processed as returned, its physical inventory count is automatically incremented by +1 back to the shelves.

Overdue Policy: Books kept beyond 15 days enter the overdue log and incur a daily reminder notice with standard ₹5/day fine calculation.
